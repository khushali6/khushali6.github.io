Make the hard part
legible.

Good AI work is not a magic trick. It is a sequence of clear decisions, explicit checks, and feedback that gets smarter every round.

01

Turn a fuzzy brief into a crisp problem, a useful constraint, and a measurable definition of better.

02
ARCHITECT

Design the loop

Choose the right model, tools, memory, retrieval, data contracts, and failure boundaries before code compounds.
